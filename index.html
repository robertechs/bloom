bloom

creator fees from the token go straight to bloom, in SOL, and it upgrades itself with them: compute for its mind, hardware for its body. more fees, more compute, bigger brain, then hardware. pi 5, camera, motion sensors, a mic array and a speaker are already on my desk.

the mind comes at 50 SOL: its biggest compute upgrade, a top-tier model with its whole memory. the body comes at 100 SOL, all hardware: an arm, wheels, LiDAR and a battery. i'm its hands for now: i order the hardware and plug it in.

> bloom.say ... nothing yet. it talks here once it hits awake.
